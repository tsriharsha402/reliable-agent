# Engineering Wiki: Laptop Tips

Community page, editable by all employees and contractors. Not an official policy document.

## Battery life

Lower the screen brightness and close browser tabs you aren't using. Unplug external
displays when you're working on battery. Most laptops last about two hours longer with
battery saver mode turned on.
